Route Two Partners

A committed capital vehicle led by a team of Stanford GSB graduate and his partner, aiming to build a leading platform in water and wastewater services by acquiring and growing exceptional companies for the long-term.

TelcoBridges

A specialized developer and manufacturer of high-performance telecommunications hardware and software. TelcoBridges provides essential gateway solutions that enable interoperability between legacy and modern communication networks for clients worldwide.

Oakmont Education

A specialized charter school management company dedicated to dropout prevention and credit recovery for at-risk students. Oakmont operates multiple campuses across Ohio and Iowa, providing crucial alternative educational pathways.

Swoogo

An agile, all-in-one event management software platform that gives organizers maximum control over their branding and event experiences. Swoogo empowers users to run webinars, in-person conferences, and hybrid events with unlimited flexibility.

Pay4Leads

A differentiated digital marketing and lead generation firm for the residential services industry. The company provides qualified, exclusive leads to thousands of contractors, with a primary focus on the tree care sector.

Union Software Group

A long term holding company that acquires, builds, and grows high-quality vertical market software businesses. Its cornerstone acquisition, BusPlanner, is the leading provider of student transportation and logistics software in Canada.

Steel River

An industrial services platform executing a disciplined buy-and-build strategy in niche verticals. Its foundational acquisition, CraneTech, specializes in the inspection, repair, and manufacturing of industrial overhead cranes.
